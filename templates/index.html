The operational blind spot

Campus waste isn't a cleaning problem. It's an operations problem.

Most institutions can't see where issues start, how long they take to fix, or what they actually cost. Circuva makes the invisible visible — and accountable.

No real-time visibility

Overflowing bins and illegal dumping go unnoticed for days because reports live in group chats and scattered email threads.

Contamination & lost targets

Segregation failures quietly degrade sustainability metrics and trigger avoidable landfill penalties.

Recurring hotspots

Closed-loop workflow

From spotted issue to verified resolution

One continuous loop connects your community, your operations team, and your administrators — with a full audit trail at every step.

  1. 01

    Spot

    Any student or staff member notices an environmental issue on campus grounds.

  2. 02

    Report

    They submit photo evidence, location, and severity in under 60 seconds — no account friction.

  3. 03

    Dispatch

    Facility leads receive prioritised dispatch and route the right worker to the right place.

  4. 04

    Resolve

    The team cleans up, uploads proof, and the incident is closed with a verified record.

  5. 05

    Verify

    The original reporter confirms the fix — locking in data integrity and impact points.
