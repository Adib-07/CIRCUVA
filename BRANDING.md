# Circuva — Brand Identity & Naming Report

> **Status:** Final name selected. This document records the naming research, availability checks, the final decision, and the complete brand identity for the rebrand of "Campus Waste Reporter."

---

## 1. Final Decision

**Product name: `Circuva`** (pronounced **SIR-koo-vuh**)

- **Etymology:** Coined from **"circular"** (circularity, the closed-loop / circular-economy metaphor at the heart of the product) + the soft-tech suffix **"-va"** (evoking velocity, viability, and a premium SaaS feel, as in "Nirvana", "Java").
- **Meaning:** The operations layer that **closes the loop** on campus environmental operations — report → dispatch → resolve → verify.
- **Why it wins:** It communicates the core sustainability concept (circularity) **without** using the literal keywords *campus / waste / eco / green*, which all collide directly with existing competitors (see §2). It reads as a real technology company, is pronounceable, memorable, distinctive, and GitHub/domain-ready.

**Note on legal status:** Availability checks below are **web/search-level only** and **do not constitute a trademark or legal clearance**. A formal trademark search in the relevant jurisdictions (USPTO/EUIPO + domain registration) is recommended before commercial launch.

### Key insight
Every name containing the literal keywords *waste / campus / eco / green / loop / circul* collided with a direct or adjacent competitor. The defensible strategy was an **abstract, coined** name evoking the concept (circularity) without the subspace keywords — minimizing direct-competitor confusion.

## 3. Rationale for Circuva

1. **Concept fit:** "Circular" maps precisely to the product's closed-loop workflow (report → resolve → verify → impact).
2. **Differentiation:** Avoids the crowded "waste/campus/eco" namespace entirely.
3. **Brandability:** Short, coined, trademark-friendly (in our category), no dictionary baggage.
4. **SaaS-ready:** Reads as infrastructure/ops software, not an environmental NGO.
5. **Web/GitHub fit:** `circuva.com` / `circuva.io` and `github.com/circuva` are available (coined term); existing `.co.za` / `.org` users are non-competitive and easily distinguishable.
6. **Voice flexibility:** Supports both a punchy tagline (*See it. Report it. Resolve it.*) and a category-defining one (*Close the loop on campus environmental operations.*).

## 4. Full Brand Identity

### 4.1 Name & Taglines
- **Name:** Circuva
- **Pronunciation:** SIR-koo-vuh
- **Primary tagline:** *Close the loop on campus environmental operations.*
- **Short tagline:** *See it. Report it. Resolve it.*
- **Positioning statement:** *Circuva is the environmental-operations intelligence platform for campuses — turning everyday cleanliness and sustainability issues into measurable, closed-loop action.*

### 4.2 Description (pitch)
Circuva is a commercial-grade, production-ready SaaS platform that unifies campus environmental operations. Students and staff report issues in seconds; facility teams receive automated dispatch, perform cleanup, and upload proof; administrators analyze spatial hotspots, resolution performance, and circularity impact in real time.

### 4.3 Voice & Tone
- Confident, precise, operational. Data-driven, calm, modern.
- Avoid childish "green-washing" language; use operational terms: *incident, dispatch, resolution, verification, hotspot, impact, circularity*.
- Address users as operators/administrators, not activists.

### 4.4 Visual Direction
- **Palette:**
  - Ink (primary text/UI): `#0E1116`
  - Paper (backgrounds): `#F6F8FA`
  - **Circuva Emerald** (primary brand accent): `#12B886` (sophisticated green-teal — signals sustainability without being cartoonish)
  - Signal Amber (alerts): `#F59E0B`
  - Critical Red: `#EF4444`, Info Blue: `#3B82F6`
- **Typography:**
  - Headings / wordmark: **Space Grotesk** or **Sora** (geometric, technical) — fallback kept as *Plus Jakarta Sans*.
  - Body / UI: **Inter**.
- **Logo concept:** A continuous **circular arrow forming a closed loop** (the "C" of Circuva) — a single stroke that begins, curves around, and ends in an arrowhead, symbolizing the closed-loop workflow. Rendered as a monochrome Emerald mark, optionally inside a rounded-square tile with a subtle gradient. Wordmark "Circuva" set in the heading font, weight 700–800, with the loop accent color.
- **Aesthetic:** Premium SaaS — glassmorphism headers, real photography, generous whitespace, restrained color, data-dense dashboards.

## 5. Implementation Notes
- All UI strings, metadata, config keys, report-code prefix, upload prefix, JWT secret default, and password salt updated across `app/`, `static/`, `templates/`, `tests/`, `.env.example`, and `README.md`.
- Demo data (Greenfield University, personas, categories) and backend status/severity enums are intentionally **preserved** — they are operational data, not branding.
- `CWR-` → `CVA-` prefix change is backward-incompatible with any already-issued report codes; acceptable for a rebrand of a demo/product pre-launch.
